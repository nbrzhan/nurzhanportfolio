Bouna Ndiaye & Jérémy Medjana

About the Founders of Comsport

Architects of the French basketball dream

Bouna Ndiaye and Jérémy Medjana are the legendary founders of Comsport, a premier sports management agency. Starting with nothing but a dream and immense dedication, they worked relentlessly to build a solid bridge between European basketball and the NBA. Today, they represent some of the highest-paid and most talented athletes in the world.

Why They Inspire Me

The movie "Мечтать не вредно" (Dream Big) deeply inspired me through its core message of brotherhood and unwavering belief. My favorite moment is when Jérémy is ready to give up, and Bouna tells him that he only needs his brother to rise to the top. Even when faced with early, humbling setbacks — like the young player who refused to stay in their modest hotel — they never lost faith in each other. Their story proves that chasing a dream with your friends and doing what you truly love is the ultimate path to success, regardless of having money or not at the start.

Key Milestones

  1. Founded Comsport with a vision to support and elevate French basketball players.
  2. Successfully negotiated Nicolas Batum's groundbreaking NBA contracts.
  3. Secured Rudy Gobert's record-breaking supermax extension in the NBA.
  4. Became the leading agency for international players entering the NBA draft.

Major Contributions

  • Created a reliable, professional pathway for European talent to reach the United States.
  • Completely changed the financial landscape for international athletes.
  • Inspired a generation by showing that ambitious dreams and hard work are essential.
